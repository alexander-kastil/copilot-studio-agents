# Good candidates for automation

## Content

- Every weekday at 8 AM, a **Daily Briefing** of meetings, priority emails and newly shared documents
- Every Friday at 4 PM, a Word **status report** on your week for your manager
- On the first of the month, a one-page **digest** of the Marketing Teams channel
- When your manager emails about supplier delays, a **summary and draft reply** for your review
- When you are @mentioned in the Aurora Outdoor Operations channel, a **one-line summary** by email

## Speaker Notes

These five examples show the work worth handing over: the same output from the same sources, on a fixed rhythm or in response to a message. The first three run on a schedule, the last two on a trigger.

Daily Briefing: every weekday at 8 AM, Cowork gathers today's meetings, unread priority emails and any documents shared with you since yesterday.

Status report: every Friday at 4 PM, it summarizes your sent emails and calendar for the week into a Word status report for your manager. The send still waits for your approval, which the next slide covers.

Monthly digest: on the first of the month, it compiles last month's project updates from the Marketing Teams channel into a one-page digest.

Email trigger: whenever your manager emails you about supplier delays, Cowork summarizes the message and drafts a reply for your review.

Teams trigger: when you are @mentioned in the Aurora Outdoor Operations channel, it emails you a one-line summary. Teams triggers can be limited to messages that mention you.
