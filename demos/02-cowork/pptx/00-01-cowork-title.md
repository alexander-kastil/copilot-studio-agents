# Microsoft 365 Copilot Cowork

## Content

- **Delegate** an outcome in plain language, and Cowork plans and runs the steps
- **Extend** it with skills, schedules and plugins
- **Approve** before anything is sent or saved

## Speaker Notes

This module covers Microsoft 365 Copilot Cowork, the agentic AI in Microsoft 365 that does work on your behalf. It has been generally available since June 2026, and access comes from a Copilot Credits spending policy rather than Frontier enrollment.

Delegate: you describe an outcome in plain language, and Cowork plans and runs the steps across your apps. Most demos share one scenario: you are the Regional Manager for Northern Europe at Aurora Outdoor, a Nordic outdoor-gear retailer, closing out Q3.

Extend: built-in and custom skills, scheduled prompts and plugins decide what Cowork can do and when it runs. The module adds them one topic at a time.

Approve: Cowork pauses before anything is sent or saved, so you stay in the loop for every action that leaves a mark. The last topic covers approvals, risk levels and admin governance.
