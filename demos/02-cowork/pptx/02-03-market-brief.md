# Producing a market brief

## Content

- **Deep Research** surveys the European outdoor market in a background task of about four minutes
- `internal-positioning.docx` turns the trends into an **aligned and exposed** gap analysis
- The **Word** skill writes AW26 Market Brief - Aurora Outdoor in four sections
- **Recommendations** rank three moves and mark one Top pick
- One background task saves the `.docx` and exports the **PDF** for leadership

## Speaker Notes

This slide shows the topic's demo, which prepares Aurora Outdoor's AW26 brand planning. The screenshot is the end of the run: Cowork's summary on the left and the PDF for leadership in the preview.

Deep Research: the first prompt asks for the trends shaping premium outdoor brands, each with its source. The chat reads Working on 1 background task, and Deep Research and Web Search appear under Plugins.

Gap analysis: the attached positioning document is held against the research in the same task. The repair programme aligns with the circular trend, while the missing sustainability metrics, the thin trail-running presence and the fleece sizing returns come back as exposed.

Word: Cowork writes an executive summary, market trends, where Aurora stands and risks to watch, about two pages. Word joins Deep Research and Web Search under Plugins.

Recommendations: the last prompt asks for a point of view, three moves in priority order with the first marked Top pick. In the class run the top pick was checking and backing up every environmental claim in market.

PDF: one background task, Add recommendations and export PDF, saves the Word file and exports the PDF with the same name. No separate PDF entry appears under Plugins.
