# Producing a market brief

## Content

- **Deep Research** surveys the European outdoor market and returns three or four cited trends
- `internal-positioning.docx` turns those trends into a **gap analysis** for Aurora
- The **Word** skill writes a two-page brief in four sections
- Three **prioritised moves** with a flagged top pick close the brief
- The Word skill **hands over to the PDF skill** for the leadership copy

## Speaker Notes

This slide previews the topic's demo, which prepares Aurora Outdoor's AW26 brand planning. Keep the side panel open while it runs, because each built-in skill activates on its own.

Deep Research: the first prompt asks for the trends shaping premium outdoor brands, each with a citation. It runs longer than a normal answer, and a strong result reads as synthesis rather than a link dump.

Gap analysis: the attached positioning document is held against the research in the same task. Look for the missing sustainability metrics and the thin trail-running presence as the exposure.

Word: Cowork writes "AW26 Market Brief - Aurora Outdoor" with an executive summary, market trends, where Aurora stands and risks to watch. It runs to roughly two pages for an executive reader.

Prioritised moves: the last prompt forces a point of view with three moves in priority order and one top pick tied to a risk. Publishing sustainability metrics to close the transparency gap is one such pick.

Hands over to the PDF skill: the updated brief is exported as a PDF with the same name, next to the .docx. It is one more built-in skill that activates because the task called for it.
