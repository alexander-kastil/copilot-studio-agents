# Takeaways

## Content

- **Describe the outcome**, and Cowork plans and acts across Microsoft 365
- A **spending policy** that selects Cowork grants access, metered in Copilot Credits
- **Skills** load on their own, and a `SKILL.md` teaches Cowork one of your recurring jobs
- **Schedules, triggers and plugins** make the work recurring and bring in outside expertise
- **You approve** every send, booking and file edit before it leaves your workspace

## Speaker Notes

This closing slide collects what the module covered, one point per phase of the path. Each point was practised in at least one demo, most of them on the Aurora Outdoor Q3 close.

Describe the outcome: Cowork plans and runs the steps across your apps. Your part is to judge the plan and steer it, as in the Q3 review deck.

Spending policy: a user can open Cowork once a spending policy that selects Cowork covers them, even with a one-credit limit. Start with a small pilot group and strict limits, then expand once you have seen real consumption.

Skills: Cowork loads the right built-in skills on its own and lists them under Plugins in the Workspace panel. A custom SKILL.md like product-pitch captures a job you repeat, and you tune it until it fires without being named.

Schedules, triggers and plugins: a prompt or custom skill can run every Friday or when an email or Teams message arrives. Plugins such as dietitian bring expertise you cannot write yourself.

You approve: Cowork pauses before anything that leaves your workspace, and a scheduled run still stops at a draft. Admins govern access, browsing and plugins for the whole organization.
