# Takeaways

## Content

- **Describe the outcome**, and Cowork plans and acts across Microsoft 365
- A **spending policy** that selects Cowork grants access, metered in Copilot Credits
- **Skills** load automatically, and a `SKILL.md` adds your own
- **Schedules, triggers and plugins** make the work recurring and bring in external data
- **You approve** before anything is sent or saved

## Speaker Notes

This closing slide collects what the module covered, one point per step of the path. Each point was practised in at least one demo.

Describe the outcome: Cowork plans and executes the steps across your apps. Your part is to judge the plan and steer it, not to drive every step.

Spending policy: a user can open Cowork once a spending policy that selects Cowork covers them, even with a one-credit limit. Start with a small pilot group and strict limits, then expand once you have seen real consumption.

Skills: Cowork loads the right built-in skills on its own and shows them in the side panel. A custom `SKILL.md` teaches it one of your own recurring workflows in plain language.

Schedules, triggers and plugins: a prompt or custom skill can run on a schedule or when an email or Teams message arrives. Plugins from the Customize page add external data and expertise.

You approve: Cowork pauses before sensitive actions, and high-risk actions still pause inside a scheduled run. On top of that, admins govern Cowork for the whole organization.
