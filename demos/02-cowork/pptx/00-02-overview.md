# Overview

## Content

- **Get started** with what Cowork is, tenant setup and how a task runs
- **Use and author skills**: the built-in catalog, the side panel and your own `SKILL.md`
- Make the work **recurring** with scheduled prompts and event-driven tasks
- Make it **extensible** with plugins that add external data and expertise
- Keep it under **human control** with approvals, risk levels and governance

## Speaker Notes

This slide walks the path through the module before the first topic starts. The six topics fall into four steps, shown left to right in the diagram.

Get started: topic 1 explains what Cowork is, how it differs from Copilot Chat, how an admin enables it and how a typical task runs. Its two demos build a Q3 business review deck and book a meeting from live calendar data.

Use and author skills: topic 2 covers the built-in skill catalog and the side panel that shows Cowork's live work. Topic 3 teaches you to write your own skill in plain language with a `SKILL.md` file.

Recurring work: topic 4 runs a prompt or custom skill on a schedule, or when an email or Teams message arrives. High-risk actions still pause for approval inside those runs.

Extensible: topic 5 adds plugins from the Customize page and shows how to upload and share your own. Plugins bring in external data and expertise.

Human control: topic 6 explains the approval model, risk levels, pause and resume, and feedback. It ends with how admins govern Cowork.
