# Steering an active task

## Content

- **Pause (soft)** finishes the current step, then pauses
- **Pause (hard)** stops immediately, mid-step
- **Resume** continues a paused task, Cancel clears the current one
- **Queued messages** sent while Cowork works can redirect it
- Rate responses and previewed documents with **thumbs up or down**, or comment inline

## Speaker Notes

You are never locked out while Cowork works. The diagram shows where each control stops a running task, measured against the step it is in.

Pause (soft): Cowork finishes the step it is on, then pauses.

Pause (hard): the task stops immediately, in the middle of the step.

Resume and Cancel: Resume continues the paused task. Cancel clears the current task.

Queued messages: anything you send while Cowork works is queued and can redirect it.
