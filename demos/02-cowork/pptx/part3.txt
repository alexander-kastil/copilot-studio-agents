# Where plugins fit
### The dietitian plugin's skills beside the built-in Word skill
- **Upload** the dietitian package under Customize > Add > Upload and publish it to Only you
- Its skills **clinical-nutrition** and **dietary-communication** draft the assessment and the client handout
- Each loads with a **Reviewed custom skill** step and joins **Plugins** next to the built-in Word
- **Word** compiles both drafts into Client Nutrition Packet - NUT-2041, the DRAFT ONLY line on top
- Sharing with specific people needs **no admin**; the whole organization waits for admin approval

# The approval model
### Cowork pauses before anything that leaves your workspace
- The card names the action as a question, here **Create calendar event?**, and shows what it will write
- **Save** approves this one action, and the task records Approved 1 action
- The arrow beside it offers **Always allow Create event** for the rest of the session
- **Medium risk** changes your content or calendar; **high risk** reaches other people
- New files in Output and saved drafts need **no approval**

# Steering an active task
### Pause, resume or cancel while Cowork works
- **Pause (soft)** finishes the current step, then pauses
- **Pause (hard)** stops immediately, mid-step
- **Resume** continues a paused task, Cancel clears the current one
- **Queued messages** sent while Cowork works can redirect it
- Rate responses and previewed documents with **thumbs up or down**, or comment inline

# Takeaways
### From a first task to staying in control
- **Describe the outcome**, and Cowork plans and acts across Microsoft 365
- A **spending policy** that selects Cowork grants access, metered in Copilot Credits
- **Skills** load on their own, and a `SKILL.md` teaches Cowork one of your recurring jobs
- **Schedules, triggers and plugins** make the work recurring and bring in outside expertise
- **You approve** every send, booking and file edit before it leaves your workspace
