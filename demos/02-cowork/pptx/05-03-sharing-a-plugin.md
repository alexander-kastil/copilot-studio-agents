# Reviewing plugin output

## Content

- A registered dietitian reviews and approves every packet before any of it reaches a client

## Speaker Notes

This slide stays with the dietitian demo for one rule that travels with every plugin. Every skill in the dietitian plugin is for drafting only, and its output is not medical nutrition therapy.

Review: before anything reaches a client, a registered dietitian reads every calculation, every lab interpretation and every handout claim, then approves or corrects it. The plugin speeds up the drafting; it does not replace the clinician.

Disclaimer: the DRAFT ONLY line at the top of the packet keeps an unreviewed draft from being mistaken for approved advice. If Cowork buries it, ask for it as the first line before anything else.

Effort: raising the reasoning effort to High or Max does not make clinical output more trustworthy. The dietitian's review is the control, not the effort level.
