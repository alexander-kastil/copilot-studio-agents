# Where plugins fit

## Content

- **Built-in skills** handle the common office tasks
- **Custom skills** from Customize or OneDrive handle your recurring workflows
- **Plugins** from Discover reach the systems and expertise your work depends on
- **Admin-deployed plugins** let IT standardize that reach across the organization
- Add a plugin under **+** > **Customize** > **Plugins**, then turn it on per conversation in **Sources & Skills**

## Speaker Notes

The previous slide showed what a plugin adds; this one shows where it lands. Cowork draws skills from five sources, and every one surfaces in the same side panel and activates automatically, based on the task.

Built-in skills: Cowork ships them for the common office tasks.

Custom skills: authored on the Customize page or saved to OneDrive, they handle your own recurring workflows.

Plugins and admin deployment: plugins from Discover reach into the systems and expertise your work depends on. Admin-deployed plugins let IT standardize that reach across the whole organization, and skills that colleagues share or that you upload join the same panel.

Installing: select + > Customize > Plugins, pick a card in Discover, review its publisher, skills and connectors, and select Add. Turn the plugin on or off per conversation in the Sources & Skills panel; on mobile the path is + > Skills.
