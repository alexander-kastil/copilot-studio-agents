# Preventing agent sprawl

## Content

- Three controls: **default environment routing**, **managed environments** and **publishing approval**
- Agents are **catalogued and reviewed** before they spread across the tenant
- Makers keep building: every published agent is **visible, owned and governed**

## Speaker Notes

A tenant can accumulate agents fast. This last governance slide shows the controls that keep that growth in view.

Three controls: default environment routing, managed environments and publishing approval sit between makers and the tenant.

Catalogued and reviewed: with these controls in place, agents are catalogued and reviewed before they spread across the tenant.

Visible, owned and governed: preventing sprawl is not about blocking makers. It makes sure every published agent is visible, owned and governed.
