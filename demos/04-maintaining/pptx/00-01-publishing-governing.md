# Publishing, Maintaining & Governing Copilot Studio Agents

## Content

- **Publish** to the channels where people already work
- **Promote** safely from development to test to production
- **Govern** the whole estate so it stays secure and countable

## Speaker Notes

Building an agent is only half the job. This module covers what comes after it works on your screen: taking it from a personal sandbox to a governed production environment.

Publish: the agent reaches people in the channels where they already work, from Teams to WhatsApp to your own website.

Promote: solutions, pipelines and source control move the agent between development, test and production without rebuilding it by hand.

Govern: data loss prevention, Microsoft Purview and admin controls keep the whole estate secure and countable as the number of agents grows.
