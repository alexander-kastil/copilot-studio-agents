# Republishing a change

## Content

- Every change reaches users only through **Publish**
- A running conversation keeps the old version until a **new session** starts
- **start over** forces the new session in Teams
- The last answer ends with the **new sentence** from the republished topic

## Speaker Notes

Publishing once is easy; the question is what users see after the next change. This chat in Teams comes from the module 3 demo, where one sentence was added to the Collect Employee Data topic and the agent was published again.

Publish: every change reaches users only through Publish, and one publish updates Teams and Microsoft 365 Copilot at once.

New session: updated content reaches users only after a new session starts, so a conversation that is already running keeps the old version.

start over: typing start over in Teams forces that new session. The Publish dialog also offers Force newest version, which moves ongoing Teams chats at once but can interrupt them.

New sentence: after start over the confirmation ends with "Ask me to collect your employee reward next.", the sentence the republish added.
