# Unmanaged developer environments

## Content

- Export your solutions or move to a **managed environment** before December 15, 2026

## Speaker Notes

Microsoft is retiring unmanaged developer environments, including the ones the free Power Apps Developer Plan creates on sign-up. The developer type stays, but only as a managed environment, one per user.

The dates: creation of unmanaged developer environments ends on November 15, 2026. Suspension starts on December 15, 2026, and deletion of suspended environments on January 15, 2027.

Admins: they filter Manage, Environments to Type Developer and Managed No and use Last activity to spot the ones in use. For each one still needed they enable Managed Environments, which needs premium licensing, or move its solutions to a sandbox.

Default environment routing: admins turn it on so every maker gets a managed developer environment of their own.

Makers: export the solutions from your developer environment before December 15, 2026. This is one more reason to build in a custom solution from the start, and a standard trial environment still lets you try premium capabilities.

Not affected: managed developer environments, including the routed ones, and production, sandbox, trial and default environments.
