# Copilot Chat and Microsoft 365 Copilot

## Content

- **Copilot Chat** is free in every commercial tenant and grounded in the web
- A **Microsoft 365 Copilot license** grounds the same chat in your emails, chats, files and meetings
- **Licensed extras**: Copilot inside Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook and Teams, plus Researcher and Analyst
- Reach it at **m365.cloud.microsoft**, in the Microsoft 365 Copilot apps, Teams, Outlook and Office

## Speaker Notes

This slide sets the baseline: what Copilot already does before you build anything yourself.

Copilot Chat: everyone in a commercial tenant gets it for free, grounded in public web content. It is the always-on AI you talk to in plain language to draft, summarize and answer questions.

Microsoft 365 Copilot license: it grounds the same chat in your emails, chats, files and meetings via Microsoft Graph. Catching up on a 40-message thread before a meeting is a typical Copilot Chat summary.

Licensed extras: Copilot is embedded in Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook and Teams, with Researcher, Analyst and custom agents. Controls grow from basic to the Copilot Control System and Copilot Analytics.

Where to reach it: in the browser at m365.cloud.microsoft, in the Microsoft 365 Copilot apps, and inside Teams, Outlook and the Office apps. In the redesigned Copilot, Home brings Chat and Cowork together in one starting point.
