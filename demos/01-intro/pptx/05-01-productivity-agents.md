# Productivity and SharePoint agents

## Content

- **Researcher**: multi-step research across work content and the web, returned as a source-cited report
- **Analyst**: reasons over your data to produce analysis, tables and charts
- **SharePoint agent**: scoped to a whole site, specific folders or selected documents
- It answers only from what the **person asking** already has permission to see
- Created with **no code**, published to SharePoint and Microsoft 365 Copilot
- **Copilot in OneDrive and SharePoint** works on the files: trends across spreadsheets, folder sorting, interactive HTML reports

## Speaker Notes

The Copilot Chat basics are pre-class homework, so the topic opens here: the first-party agents a Microsoft 365 Copilot license brings with nothing to build.

Researcher: it runs multi-step research across your work content and the web and returns a structured, source-cited report. It fits a cited market scan that combines your files and the web.

Analyst: it reasons over your data to produce analysis, tables and charts you can act on.

SharePoint agent: an assistant scoped to the content you choose, a whole site, specific folders or selected documents. It only surfaces what the person asking already has permission to see, which makes it the fit for policy questions over a document library.

No code: you create one from a site or from selected files, then publish it so people can chat with it in SharePoint and in Microsoft 365 Copilot.

Copilot in OneDrive and SharePoint: it became generally available in October 2026 and works where the files live. Select several spreadsheets and ask for trends and charts, point it at a crowded folder to sort it into a structure, or turn existing files into an interactive HTML report that you refine with follow-up prompts. OneDrive opens that HTML, and any Markdown output, for editing, anchored comments and live coauthoring.
