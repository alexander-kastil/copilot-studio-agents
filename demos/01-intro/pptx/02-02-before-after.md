# A business process before and after agents

## Content

- Today a person gathers files, reads emails, checks the CRM and **assembles a summary**
- With an agent, the review becomes **a goal you hand off**
- The agent calls tools and knowledge with **the same instructions every run**
- Time to result drops from hours to **minutes, plus a review**

## Speaker Notes

The clearest way to see the value of agents is to compare one everyday process run by hand and run with an agent.

Today: a quarterly account review means a person gathering files, reading emails, checking a CRM and assembling a summary across an afternoon. The result varies by who runs it.

A goal you hand off: with an agent, the same review becomes a goal, and the person reviews and approves the result.

Same instructions every run: the agent gathers the data by calling tools and knowledge, so the process stays consistent.

Minutes, plus a review: the person does not leave the loop. Their job moves from doing every step to defining the goal and judging the output.
