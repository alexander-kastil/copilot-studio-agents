# From copilot to agent

## Content

- Copilot Chat answers **one question at a time** while you do the planning
- Cowork takes the **outcome** you describe and proposes the plan
- It works the steps itself, here a six-slide Q3 review built from three files
- The result comes back **for your review**, with a list of what to check before you present

## Speaker Notes

Topic one ends on the step from copilot to agent, which is autonomy. The screenshot comes from the first demo of the Cowork module, where a regional manager at Aurora Outdoor needs the Q3 review deck for Thursday.

One question at a time: in Copilot Chat you drive the work turn by turn and do the planning yourself.

The outcome: in Cowork you attach three files and name the result you want, and Cowork proposes a plan before it builds anything. An agent is a copilot given a goal, the tools to pursue it, and a loop to keep going until the goal is met.

Working the steps: Cowork read the sales workbook, the regional notes and last quarter's deck, then built Aurora Northern Europe - Q3 Review with the same six slides as the Q2 deck.

For your review: the reply on the left lists what changed on each slide and what to fix before you present, such as the hiring-cost placeholder that no file could fill. How that loop works, and what an agent is made of, is the next topic.
