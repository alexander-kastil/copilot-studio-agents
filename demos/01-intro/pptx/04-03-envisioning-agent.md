# Envisioning an agent

## Content

- Turns a vague wish ("we want an AI for support") into a **scoped, buildable agent**
- **Canvas**: the user, the outcome, the knowledge, the tools, what a human approves, how you know it works
- Each need maps to a **capability**: knowledge source, tool, approval, channel, evaluations
- **Envision, map, then build**, in that order

## Speaker Notes

Once the paths are clear, this slide covers the habit that keeps you out of the wrong one. The most expensive mistake is building the wrong thing well.

Scoped agent: envisioning is a short, structured pass that turns a vague wish such as "we want an AI for support" into a buildable agent. Fill it in with a stakeholder before you open Copilot Studio.

Canvas: it asks who the user is, what outcome they need, what knowledge and tools the agent uses, what a human must approve, and how you know it works. In the support example, refunds over a threshold need approval, and first-contact resolution and CSAT measure success.

Capability: each need maps to a concrete Microsoft capability. Policy documents become a knowledge source, a live order lookup a tool, refund approval a human-in-the-loop step, Teams a channel, and the go-live proof agent evaluations and test sets.

Order: envision, map, then build. You will do this end to end in the final module on designing business processes.
