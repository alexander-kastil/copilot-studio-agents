# Extending Copilot

## Content

- **Out of the box**: Copilot answers from the web and, with a license, your Graph content
- **Extensibility** adds your custom systems, line-of-business data and processes
- **Ground Copilot** in more of your content with connectors and knowledge sources
- **Build a focused agent** with its own instructions, knowledge and tools

## Speaker Notes

This slide opens the topic with the reason extensibility exists. Microsoft offers several ways to extend Copilot, and picking one is a design decision.

Out of the box: Microsoft 365 Copilot answers from the web and, with a license, from your Graph content. It does not know your custom systems, your line-of-business data or your specific processes.

Extensibility: this is how you close that gap. You add knowledge, connect tools and shape behaviour so Copilot works on your organisation's terms.

Ground Copilot: connectors and knowledge sources let it answer from systems it could not see before.

Build a focused agent: an agent with its own instructions, knowledge and tools handles a specific job end to end. Agent Builder, Copilot Studio and pro-code all build this kind of agent.
