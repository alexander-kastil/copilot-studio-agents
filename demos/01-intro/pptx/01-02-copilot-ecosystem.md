# The Microsoft 365 Copilot ecosystem

## Content

- What people use and what makers build both stand on **Microsoft Graph** and the tools that reach other systems

## Speaker Notes

Copilot is a layer that spans the platform, and the pieces in this course sit at different levels of it.

Where people work: the experiences layer is what end users touch, Copilot Chat on the web and in Teams, the agentic workspace Cowork, and Copilot inside Word, Excel and Outlook.

Where makers build: the build layer is where makers and developers assemble agents, low-code in Copilot Studio or with SharePoint and Agent Builder agents.

Microsoft Graph: both layers stand on a shared foundation, and Graph grounds Copilot in your emails, files, chats and meetings.

Tools, connectors and MCP servers: they let agents reach beyond Graph into other systems, which is where the later modules on tools pick up.
