# From Copilot to Cowork

## Content

- An agent is a Copilot with a **goal**, **tools** and a **loop** until it is met

## Speaker Notes

Topic one ends on the step from Copilot to agent, and that step is autonomy.

Copilot Chat: the AI thought partner. You ask one question, Chat answers that question, and you merge the answers into the result yourself. You do the planning, one question at a time.

Cowork: the AI colleague who actually does the work. You describe the outcome, Cowork breaks the goal into tasks and selects skills, acts across Outlook, Teams, Word, Excel and PowerPoint, and hands back documents and sent messages for your review.

The definition: an agent is a Copilot given a goal, the tools to pursue it, and a loop to keep going until the goal is met. How that loop works, and what an agent is made of, is the next topic.
