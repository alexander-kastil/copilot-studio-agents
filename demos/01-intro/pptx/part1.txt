# Copilot, agent and assistant
### Three terms the course uses with fixed meanings
- **Assistant**: any AI that helps a person complete work in natural language
- **Copilot**: embedded in an app you already use, grounded in your work content
- **Agent**: a copilot with a specific job, its own knowledge, instructions and tools
- With a copilot the human asks; with an agent the human **sets a goal**

# From copilot to agent
### Cowork turns an outcome into a finished deck
- Copilot Chat answers **one question at a time** while you do the planning
- Cowork takes the **outcome** you describe and proposes the plan
- It works the steps itself, here a six-slide Q3 review built from three files
- The finished deck comes back **for your review**, with a list of what to check before you present

# Agent, model and harness
### What you design, what reasons, what coordinates
- The **agent** is what you design: instructions, knowledge, tools and boundaries
- The **model** reasons and writes, and models differ in speed, cost and depth
- The **harness** coordinates: when to call the model, what to send, which step runs next
- Same model, different harness: **a different agent**
