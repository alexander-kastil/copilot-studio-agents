# Draft and published version

## Content

- You edit a draft, colleagues chat with the **published** version, and sharing decides who may chat at all

## Speaker Notes

Two rules decide what colleagues experience once the agent is live.

Draft and published: the test panel always runs the latest draft, while channels run the last published version. Every change reaches users only through Publish.

Sessions: running conversations don't switch versions, and in most channels a session ends after 30 minutes of inactivity. In Teams, start over resets the session at once; otherwise an update can take up to an hour to arrive.

Sharing: installing the agent gives nobody permission to chat with it. Share it with a security group as End user access, shown as Viewer, and Show to my teammates and shared users lists it in the Teams app store without admin approval.

Rollout: Learn's advice is to publish for yourself first, test the published version in Teams, share with a small group and submit to the organization last.
