# Takeaways

## Content

- Write **instructions** that state the job, the limits and how a conversation ends
- Give every knowledge source and topic a **description** the orchestrator can choose by
- Trust an answer once its **citation** opens the source
- Keep fixed paths in **topics**, logic in **Power Fx** and multi-field input on **Adaptive Cards**
- Users chat with the **published** version, and only once you share it

## Speaker Notes

Close the module with the habits it built. The photo shows where CS-HR Buddy's conversation ends: a dinner voucher or theatre tickets for an identified employee.

Instructions: a blank agent answers anything. Purpose, guidelines, steps, error handling and a closing rule kept the onboarding assistant on onboarding questions.

Descriptions: generative orchestration reads the description of every source and topic to decide what answers, so HR Documents and Sightseeing each got their own.

Citations: with ungrounded answers allowed, a model answer sounds as right as a grounded one, so the citation is the proof.

Topics, Power Fx and cards: a global variable let two HR Buddy topics share who the employee is, three formulas made the expense claim ready to save, and one card took the canteen's demand for nine dishes.

Published and shared: the test panel runs the draft, Teams runs the published version, and sharing decides who may chat. The next module gives these agents tools, starting with the canteen order prompt and the expense claims table in Dataverse.
