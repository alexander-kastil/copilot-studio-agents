# How people hand it work

## Content

- Colleagues hand it work in Teams, Outlook and Office comments; the agent prepares, chases and summarizes, and people decide priorities and sign off

## Speaker Notes

Colleagues reach an agent with an account through the tools they already use. The photo shows the part that stays with people: a finance team signing off on the weekly review its agent prepared.

Teams and Outlook: people ask questions, assign work or contribute updates there. They can also delegate a process task by Teams message or email and keep working.

Comments in Office documents: an @mention in Word, Excel or PowerPoint brings the agent into a document. During the preview this supports limited scenarios only.

People decide: the agent prepares, chases and summarizes, while people set priorities and sign off. In Microsoft's example a finance lead's budget agent prepares the weekly review each Tuesday, and the finance team contributes updates through the tools it already uses.

Attribution: the agent acts with its own credentials, so an admin sees which actions it took, under which identity and with which permissions. Nobody approves a scheduled run as it happens, which is why the human checkpoints in the instructions matter.
