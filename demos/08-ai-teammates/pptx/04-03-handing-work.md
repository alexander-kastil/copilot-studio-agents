# How people hand it work

## Content

- Invoke it in **Teams and Outlook** to ask questions, assign work or contribute updates
- **@mention** it in comments in Word, Excel and PowerPoint
- The agent prepares, chases and summarizes; **people** decide priorities and sign off
- **Attribution**: work done with its own credentials is attributed to the agent

## Speaker Notes

Colleagues reach an agent with an account through the tools they already use. This slide covers how they hand it work and how they stay in charge.

Teams and Outlook: people ask questions, assign work or contribute updates there. They can also delegate a process task by Teams message or email and keep working.

@mention in comments: in Word, Excel and PowerPoint, an @mention brings the agent into a document. During the preview it supports limited scenarios only.

People decide: replies and comments are how people add judgment. In Microsoft's example a finance lead's budget agent prepares the weekly review each Tuesday while the team contributes updates, as the two colleagues in the photo do.

Attribution: the agent acts with its own credentials, so an admin sees which actions it took, under which identity and with which permissions. Nobody approves a scheduled run as it happens, which is why the human checkpoints in the instructions matter.
