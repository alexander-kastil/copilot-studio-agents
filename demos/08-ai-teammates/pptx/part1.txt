# Autopilot or an agent account
### Two ways Microsoft 365 delivers a teammate today
- **Autopilot**: a user describes the name, role and goal
- **Agent account**: a maker configures instructions, tools and triggers in Copilot Studio
- Both end in an **identity** that admins govern in Agent 365 and Entra
- **Private preview**: Autopilot has no general availability date yet
- **Usage-based billing**: Cowork, Code and Autopilot consume Copilot Credits

### Name, role, goal and the boundaries you set

Name: Supplier Review Lead
Role: Run the quarterly supplier review for the procurement team.
Goal: Every active supplier has a scorecard, a review meeting, and recorded actions by the end of the quarter.
Boundaries: Contact suppliers only through the procurement shared mailbox. Ask me before you move a meeting with a supplier executive.
Escalate: Any supplier with two missed deliveries or an open quality incident.

# Heartbeat and automations
### How Autopilot keeps working in the background
- **Heartbeat**: one prompt every 15 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour or 2 hours, within your work hours
- **Automations**: schedule-triggered, condition-triggered or one-shot
- **Interactive conversation**: briefing, steering and reviewing results
- Background modes run under a **more restrictive permission policy**
- Mechanics documented for **Microsoft Scout**, the preview Autopilot grew out of

# Approvals and untrusted content
### What pauses for you and what stays data
- Before it **sends an email, posts in Teams or runs a command**, it pauses and shows its plan
- **Always allow** adds the pattern to the auto-approve list
- **Capability categories** (file system, shell, browser, Microsoft 365) switch on or off
- **Sensitive locations** can always require approval
- **Untrusted content** from emails, web pages and Teams messages stays data

# Publishing an agent with an account
### What the account provisions at publish
- Add the **account** to a new agent before its first publish
- **Publish** provisions the mailbox, Teams presence, directory entry and Entra Agent IDs
- Each agent takes one **Frontier for AI Teammates** license; with none free, publishing fails
- **Agent 365** governs the account; admins manage the Entra Agent ID separately
- Colleagues find it in the **Teams people picker** and the directory
