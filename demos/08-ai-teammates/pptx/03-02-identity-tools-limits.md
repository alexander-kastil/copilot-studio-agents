# Identity and preview limits

## Content

- The **email address** starts with your alias, and the agent reports to you
- In the preview neither can change, and the **address** stays fixed once you publish
- **No external users** and **no co-authoring**: it writes new documents and leaves existing ones as they are
- It replies only in conversations with up to **250 people**
- It cannot read files labeled **Confidential** or **Internal Only**

## Speaker Notes

Some account decisions are final once you publish, and the preview limits shape how you design the process. The photo shows the step the account adds: the agent joins the org chart under the person it reports to.

Email address: your own alias is a fixed prefix, and the agent reports to you. Pick a name your colleagues will recognize in a mail thread, because that name is how the process will know it.

Fixed after publish: during the preview you cannot change the prefix or the reporting relationship. The email address cannot change after you publish.

No external users, no co-authoring: the agent does not respond to anyone outside its tenant. It generates new documents but does not edit existing ones in place.

250 people: it replies only in conversations with up to 250 people. The error reference for this limit is MCS-4043.

Protected documents: files labeled Confidential or Internal Only are out of reach. A review agent that writes a fresh document each cycle works today; one that edits a shared tracker in place does not.
